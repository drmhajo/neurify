# Neurify Brand Asset Notes

The approved square launcher master uses the neural-path **N** mark only on a deep navy ground, with white, clinical turquoise, and gold connections. It remains the source for the text-free iOS launcher, favicon, and Android adaptive icon assets.

The approved humanist wordmark combines the neural-path mark with the navy **Neurify** name. It is exported as a transparent bundled asset for the in-app loading experience, rather than being placed inside the small launcher icon.

The exported 1024 px launcher asset keeps the neural-path mark centered and clear against the deep navy field. The Android adaptive foreground exports the mark with transparent margins and a 720 px safe content area, allowing the Android system mask to crop it without clipping the connection nodes.
